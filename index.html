A softer start for hard moments

You do not need to be yelled at to begin.

ADHD can make task initiation, working memory, sequencing, sensory load, and recovery from an imperfect chore genuinely difficult. Chore Calm turns that moment into a small, steady next step.

The promise

Calm coaching, without threats or guilt.

◌

No yelling

Chore Calm does not threaten, shame, punish, or use guilt to create urgency. It can offer a calm reminder and a clear next step.

Honest boundary: other people's anger and yelling are not controlled by you, and this concept cannot control them.

↺

Recovery is part of the plan

Missed a step, made a mess, or ran out of energy? The coach helps you pause, choose one repair, and return without a lecture.

✦

Support, not a cure

This is a concept for gentle scaffolding. It does not cure ADHD, replace human support, or decide what is best for your life.

    A concept with limits

    The goal is one kind next step.

    Chore Calm is a concept page, not the primary app or runtime, and not a playable game. It is a small design study for support that is specific, calm, and honest about what it cannot change.
