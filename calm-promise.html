What the companion does

A promise you can inspect, not just a slogan.

♡

It does not yell

Messages stay calm even when the chore is late, messy, repeated, or done incorrectly.

 ∞

It does not get annoyed

There is no annoyance meter, no sarcasm counter, and no punishment for needing the steps again.

 ↺

It allows repeated resets

Messes can happen again and again. The companion returns to one safe repair instead of keeping a tally.

 ↗

It reteaches forgotten chores

Forgetting is a prompt to explain the routine again, not proof that someone deserves a lecture.

 ⟲

It protects the next step

The visual system keeps one next action clear, with pause, skip, retry, and ask-for-help paths.

 !

It stays honest

The companion cannot control another person's yelling or guarantee a safe home. It says that plainly.
Relationship boundary: the concept never uses threats or relationship ultimatums, and it never replaces human support or emergency help.
