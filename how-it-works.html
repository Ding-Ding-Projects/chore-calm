Five visual beats

This companion never needs a dramatic entrance.

The graphics stay soft because the job is to lower the amount of alarm around the task, not add more.

01

Notice

Name what is happening without calling it a character flaw. A full sink is information, not a moral score.

 02

Break it down

Choose one handle, sock, towel, or safe patch. The rest can stay visible without demanding everything at once.

 03

Coach

Explain the next movement in plain words and keep the pace with the person doing the chore.

 04

Recover

If a small detail goes wrong, the companion offers one safe repair. No raised voice. No relationship ultimatum.

 05

Calm completion

Finish one part, pause, or ask for help. The plan stays useful even when the room is not perfect.

 ∞

Try again

Unlimited patience means the teaching loop can restart without an annoyance ledger.
Important boundary: the companion can offer calm support. It cannot control another person's anger or guarantee that nobody else will yell.
